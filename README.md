# mod_ohmslaw

Ohm's Law is a Moodle activity for calculating electrical relationships from any two positive quantities among voltage
(V), current (I), resistance (R) and power (P).

## How it works

The learner enters any two known values and the calculator immediately determines the other two in the browser. Besides
the final numbers, it shows the calculation steps and the equivalent V/I/R/P relationships used to reach the result.

The activity also shows conductance and common unit conversions, making it useful both as a quick calculator and as a
visual aid while teaching the relationships between the electrical quantities.

Student-entered calculator values are not stored.
